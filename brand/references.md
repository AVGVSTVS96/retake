# references

Bassim's saves (me.db on the Mac, searched Oct 10 2026 for waveform, voice memos, record button and audio app) had nothing on audio apps or recorders, so this leans on the references his other brands already took from his saves, and on the product itself, which here is the strongest reference of all.

## The one studied deeply

**retake's own recording screen** (`~/Developer/voice-memos/shots/raw/iphone-18-pro-max-recording-full-*.png`, and the motion captures). Every motif is lifted from it and measured, not imitated: the record button's ring and disc, the 2px playhead with a dot at each end, bars about 2pt wide on a 4.5–5pt pitch with a dotted line for silence, trim's yellow selection, the system red, blue and grays sampled from the PNGs. The hero strip's record and stop moments are read from the clip's frames. The brand is the app's parts, rearranged.

## From Bassim's saves and the brands he approved

1. **@oliverhamrin on Jacqueline Casey, "take a simple idea and take it seriously"**. https://x.com/oliverhamrin/status/2091117071308370089. The mark is one idea, the button mid-morph, drawn with care rather than a clever combination.
2. **@kazdenc, brand tools in code**. https://x.com/kazdenc/status/2099603500220973096. `logo/gen.mjs` builds every logo file and the app icon from the font outlines; every image asset is HTML rendered by a script.
3. **Apple and iOS for app and phone pages** (from `refs.md`: the look Bassim picked for phone pages). The grouped-gray paper, the white sheets, real screenshots in a plain bezel, nothing glassy on the page.
4. **@emilkowalski's animation vocabulary** and **Rauno's interface guidelines**. https://animations.dev/vocabulary · https://interfaces.rauno.me/. Motion follows state, reduced motion gets the settled state, presses scale to 0.97, no scroll hijacking.
5. **"Just width + line height can completely change your design"**, @AdamBartas. https://x.com/AdamBartas/status/2093382621623681449. Measures held to 30–34em and the lede at 1.45.
6. **seqno's brand** (`~/dev/seqno-site/brand`). The shape of this folder, and its lesson on dark mode: build dark around the dark screenshots. Here that means the app's pure black.

## Deliberately not taken

- **Apple's own marks, footage and screenshots.** Never on the page, even where they'd prove a point; the timing chart is drawn from frame counts instead.
- **Liquid Glass on the page.** The app uses it; the page doesn't, because blur and glass on the page are on the never list and would read as an Apple pastiche.
- **Waveform type and microphones**, the audio-app clichés (direction C was drawn to check, and passed).
- **One lone accent.** Red alone went flat; red, trim yellow and blue each carry their meaning from the app.
- **Mono labels, tracked caps eyebrows, hairline broadsheet.** Nothing here is a terminal or a newspaper.
- **Serif display type**, rejected for hex and wrong for an iOS app.
